# Sasti-Sehat

**Sasti-Sehat** is an AI-powered healthcare price transparency platform designed to empower patients with transparent pricing data. It enables users to compare treatment costs across hospitals, estimate medical expenses, upload and analyze medical bills, and make informed healthcare decisions before receiving treatment.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🏥 **Treatment & Hospital Cost Comparison**: Search and compare treatment costs across different hospitals and clinics.
- 📄 **AI Medical Bill Analysis**: Upload medical bills or quotes to detect hidden charges, overpricing, or discrepancies using AI.
- 💡 **Expense Estimator**: Predict out-of-pocket medical expenses based on insurance, location, and procedure.
- 🤖 **AI Healthcare Assistant**: Get intelligent guidance on treatment choices, cost ranges, and financial assistance programs.

## 🏗️ Architecture & Technology Stack

Sasti-Sehat is built using a modern full-stack architecture:
- **Frontend**: React.js
- **Backend**: Node.js / Express.js
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **AI Services**: OCR & LLM-assisted bill parsing / cost estimation
